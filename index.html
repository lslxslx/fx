Moving averages: choose SMA (simple), EMA (exponential) or DEMA (double exponential) and the number of days (weeks / months). Up to 10.
Bollinger Band ® calculation by default is based on 20-day (week / month) simple moving average.
Moving Average Convergence/Divergence (MACD) calculation by default is based on 8-, 17- and 9-day (week / month) exponential moving average.
Rate of Change (ROC) calculation by default is based on 12-day (week / month) price change.
Relative Strength Index (RSI) calculation by default is based on 14-day average.
Stochastic (Stoch) calculation by default is based on the 14-day (week / month) range of closing prices; %D is the 3-period average of %K.
Fibonacci: retracement (0, 23.6, 38.2, 50, 61.8, 78.6 and 100%) between the highest and the lowest close of the visible part of the chart; 0% is on whichever came last.
Signals: ▲ / ▼ mark where the faster of the first two moving averages crosses above / below the slower one (with a single moving average: price against it), and where the MACD line crosses its signal line.

Zoom with Ctrl / Cmd + wheel (two fingers on touch screens), drag to move, double-click to reset. Drag the space between two panels to change their height.
Use the secondary click to draw a (trend) line. Click a line to select it (drag its ends to move them); press Delete or Backspace to delete the selected line.
Hold down the alt key (option on mac) and use the secondary click to draw a Fibonacci retracement between two points (0% at the second point); select, move and delete it like a trend line.
Hold down the shift key and click to add a horizontal (support / resistance) level. Click a level to select it, drag it up or down to move it, and press Delete or Backspace to delete it.
Settings, lines and levels are remembered in this browser.
